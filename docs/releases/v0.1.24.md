# DSpatch 0.1.24

A Linux-only pre-release with two walk fixes:

- **Colours (DSP-202).** A walk's code panes mark each place's changed lines, and only the arriving place's lines flash.
- **Delegation (DSP-203).** Delegating a walk to the agent mandates exactly the walk's changed lines, and by default only those you have not decided yet.

No Windows installer or Windows host package is published; the Windows work (DSP-182) is still open.

## A walk pane marks its own places' changed lines (DSP-202)

- **Every pane marks its own places,** active or not:
  - added lines light green, removed lines light red, of about the same strength;
  - the next pane shows its place in these colours before you arrive at it;
  - before this, only the active pane was marked strongly, and the faint colours were barely visible.
- **Changed lines outside a place** are drawn weaker but still clearly visible.
- **Unchanged context stays plain.** It has the same background inside and outside a place; the grey plate on unchanged lines is gone.
- **The gold arrival flash** covers only the arriving place's changed lines, never its unchanged context. Drawing or re-rendering a pane does not start it.
- **One definition of a place's lines.** The marking, the flash and *Accept*, *Exempt* and *Withdraw* use the same lines.
  - A place on a replacement now includes the old lines the new ones replace, as the walk marks them. In 0.1.23 a place at To decided only about its new lines.
  - Other removals in the same change are still not the place's.
- **Unchanged:** the diff window's colours, navigation, panning and scrolling. The colours show which lines belong to a place; they decide nothing and count nothing.

## Delegating a walk mandates exactly its changed lines (DSP-203)

**The fault.** In 0.1.23, *Delegate this walk to the agent…* mandated every whole change the walk passed. A walk over lines 151–159 of a new 417-line file delegated all 417 lines. The mandate is now the union of the walk places' changed lines, the same lines the walk marks and *Accept* decides about: 9 lines in that example.

**Two choices,** each with its line count shown before anything starts:
- **Review remaining lines in this walk (N of M changed lines)** is the default. It leaves out lines you have accepted or exempted, and lines of an agent review you have accepted. An agent assessment still awaiting your decision does not count as decided.
- **Review all lines in this walk (M changed lines)** is a complementary review. It changes none of your decisions.

When no line remains, the default is disabled, and no empty job can start.

**What the stored mandate governs.** The mandate is saved when the job starts. It governs:
- what the agent is told;
- which lines its answer can record;
- the report's count;
- coverage.

The agent may read whole files, callers and tests; that does not widen the mandate.

**Your decisions stand.**
- The agent's assessment never replaces or withdraws a decision of yours, including one you make while the job runs.
- **In the diff window,** your decision stays the main band. The agent's assessment of the same line shows as a thin edge beside it: dotted while it awaits your decision, solid once you accept it. Hovering names both.
- **In the walk,** a place says that the agent also assessed its lines and that your decision stands.
- **No line counts twice.**

**Older jobs.** A walk delegated with 0.1.23 or earlier keeps the mandate it was given: the whole changes the walk passed. Its report says so. No narrower mandate is inferred for it.

## Install or upgrade

Install `dspatch_0.1.24_amd64.deb` on Debian or Ubuntu x64:

```bash
sudo apt install ./dspatch_0.1.24_amd64.deb
```

Installing over an earlier version keeps `~/.config/DSpatch`.

**A host left running by 0.1.15 to 0.1.23.** 0.1.24 changes no host method, so its host contract is the one those versions share. Such a host, left running by a closed window, is kept, and at start DSpatch offers **Restart host**. Press it before you delegate a walk.
- An older host does not keep a delegation's choice of remaining or all lines.
- The job's lines are still exactly the walk's, but its report then calls it an older delegation.
- The colours need no restart.

**A host started by 0.1.14 or earlier** cannot serve this window. At start DSpatch asks **Stop it and continue** or **Quit DSpatch**.

`dspatch-host-0.1.24-<commit>-linux-x64.tar.gz`, `host-manifest.json` and `SHA256SUMS` are published with this release.

## Verification

Checked before publication; details are in DSP-202, DSP-203, `docs/evaluations/dsp-202/` and `docs/evaluations/dsp-203/`.

**Tests.** Type checks and the build pass. The review group passes on Linux CI. Targeted tests cover:
- the colour cases A–D;
- the 417-line delegation (9 lines);
- remaining lines after partial manual review;
- overlapping places;
- decisions made while a job runs;
- older jobs.

**Window trials on Linux under Xvfb,** with the reviewing agent simulated:
- **walk marking:** drawn colours checked, and the flash caught at 150 ms;
- **walk delegation:** 4 of 9 lines remaining after you accept 5; then all 9, with your lines staying yours;
- **review thread trial:** threads, walk panning and the active mark under the new rule;
- **the Accept scope trial** and **the preferences and About trial,** again.

**Window trials on Windows:** the marking, delegation and Accept trials.

**Not established:**
- distributions other than Ubuntu and Debian;
- arm64;
- Windows packages;
- the quality of a real agent's review: the trials show the mandate and the bookkeeping only.
