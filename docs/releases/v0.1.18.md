# DSpatch 0.1.18

A Linux-only pre-release in which a delegated agent review is material and recommendation, and the person decides (DSP-193). No Windows installer or Windows host package is published; the Windows work (DSP-182) is still open.

## A delegated review no longer approves anything by itself (DSP-193)

- **The agent assesses, you decide.** A delegated review's result is the agent's: what it examined, what it assessed acceptable, its findings, open questions and verification gaps. Nothing of it counts as reviewed until you accept the assessment in the report (*Accept the agent's assessment*); *Do not accept* and *Withdraw your decision* complete the choice, and the decision is kept in the review's record apart from the agent's result. Accepting closes no finding and answers no question: the threads stay as they are. Before 0.1.18 a finished delegated review counted its approved lines as reviewed at once; that was a state, not only a wording.
- **The report says what is true.** It leads with the state (*Agent review complete — awaiting your assessment*, *Accepted by you on …*, *Not accepted by you*), then what the agent examined and assessed as counts of the part's changed lines, with no exact figure where the changed lines are not known, then what remains from the threads (open problems, questions, verification gaps). "Approved by the agent" is gone from every view; the agent's lines are assessed acceptable, and reviewed through a delegation you accepted once you have.
- **In the strip and the file list.** A line the agent assessed but you have not decided on is drawn hollow in the agent's colour and counted as nothing; a file's percentage says how many lines are assessed and awaiting you. A line reviewed through an accepted delegation keeps the agent's colour and names the agent.
- **Older reviews.** A delegated review made by an earlier version that you never decided about reads as the agent's assessment awaiting you, never as a decision of yours; your own marks and the earlier reports are kept as they were, with their origin. Files that were counted as handled through such a review are counted again once you accept the assessment.
- **Step keeps its explanation; Review holds the report.** In the walk window the step's explanation stays the main content of Step. The walk's delegated review, its report and the delegation control are in the step's Review section, folded, once for the whole walk; the section lists the agent's findings at the step's places, folded, and says how many of the walk's others are in the report. A result that arrives while you read changes no tab, focus or scroll position and opens nothing. The report's lines select a finding's one card rather than opening a second copy.
- **Findings fold.** In Step and in the diff view, findings are folded by default, open and close one at a time, and are not reopened by updates. In a narrow panel a finding's title has a line of its own with its status, kind and reply count below it, and long file references wrap without widening the panel.

The delegated-review instruction the agent receives says the same: its "approved" lines are an assessment for the person, who accepts or does not; it is not the person's approval. The reference `docs/REVIEW_COVERAGE_AND_CODE_WALKS.md` and `docs/VISION.md` section 3.4 record the decision of 10 November 2026.

## Install or upgrade

Install `dspatch_0.1.18_amd64.deb` on Debian or Ubuntu x64 (`sudo apt install ./dspatch_0.1.18_amd64.deb`). Installing over an earlier version keeps `~/.config/DSpatch`.

0.1.18 changes no host method, so its host contract is the one 0.1.15 to 0.1.17 share. A host that one of those versions started and that a closed window left running is kept: at start DSpatch says a restart is needed to finish updating and offers **Restart host**; press it before delegating or preparing walks, since the host records the agent's results and your decisions. A host that 0.1.14 or earlier started cannot serve this window: at start DSpatch asks **Stop it and continue** or **Quit DSpatch**, and this release's host then starts.

`dspatch-host-0.1.18-<commit>-linux-x64.tar.gz`, `host-manifest.json` and `SHA256SUMS` are published with this release.

## Verification

Checked before publication (details in DSP-193): type checks and the build; the review test group and the tests of the pair model, the delegated-review view, the agent jobs and the threads, with new cases for the counting rule, the decision through the host, older records and the report's wording; the delegated-review trial in real windows with the simulated provider (`app/scripts/delegation-trial.mjs`: the report folded and awaiting, assessed lines counted as nothing, findings folded and opening one by one, a comment reopening nothing, accept, withdraw, reject and accept again, the walk's report in Review once and folded with the tab, scroll position and focus unmoved on arrival, the step's findings folded, the report's line selecting one card, the panel floating and docked at a narrow width, a restart with the decision marks removed from the record read as an older record); the follow preparation trial and the thread trial with their wording updated.

Not established: anything with a real provider in this release (the trial's reviews are simulated and say nothing of an agent's review quality); `linux-verify` and the published-package trial, reported in the task; Windows, other distributions than Ubuntu and Debian, and arm64.
