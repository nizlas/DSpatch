## Purpose

Combine the parts made in parallel into the working copy and establish, by running checks, whether the whole works.

## Boundaries

Preserve existing work. Never delete a temporary branch. A clean merge is not evidence that the parts work together.

## Delivery

Say what you merged, what conflicted and how you resolved it, what you ran and what it showed, and what is still wrong.
