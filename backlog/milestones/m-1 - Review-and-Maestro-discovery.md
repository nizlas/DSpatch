## Description

After Phase 0 and DSP-6, use actual experience, HCI research and prior work to decide the next product direction with Niclas. [DSP-11](../tasks/dsp-11%20-%20Research-code-understanding-and-agree-next-direction.md) tracks the first bounded study.

Compare Niclas's five emerging review concepts with the evidence, explore a small selection through sketches or prototypes, and consider their relationship to Maestro loop orchestration. The concepts are hypotheses; no feature, interaction design, editor technology or agent topology is selected by this milestone.

The result is an evidence-based direction decision with Niclas and a bounded next experiment. See the [research brief](../../docs/REVIEW_RESEARCH.md). Completing this discovery is not required to close Phase 0, and this milestone does not implement the later product phases.
