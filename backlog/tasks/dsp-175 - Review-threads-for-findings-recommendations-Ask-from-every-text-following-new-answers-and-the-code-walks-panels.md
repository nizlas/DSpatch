## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Niclas's assignment of 5 November 2026, after trying 0.1.7 on real code on Linux. Review works on the whole, but:

1. **Shared review threads.** After a delegated review of a walk the diff view says "1 open finding" and the finding cannot be read there; findings, questions and "things not established" are scattered. Present the agent's findings as comment threads at the relevant code in the diff view, reachable from the walks and their Step/Discussion views, with one identity and one store (no copies with different status). A thread shows kind, title, origin and content; takes comments and replies; collapses to one line with status, title and the number of replies; when chosen shows and highlights its code, opening and scrolling to it if needed; keeps its history and status across reload, reopening and a host reconnect; and updates consistently in every open view. The open-findings count opens the open threads. Anchors name file, revision, diff side and line range; removed lines and several references are handled; an anchor that cannot be found says so and marks no arbitrary code; findings without a safe line anchor show at file or review level.
2. **Recommendation and decision.** Each new finding, question or verification gap from the agent carries what was observed and on what evidence, a recommended action, a short rationale and remaining uncertainty, and a concrete next step when more investigation is needed. The recommendation can be a code change, a test, a measurement, a question to the author, or keeping the code with a reason; a change is never forced without evidence. Primary choices *Accept recommendation* and *Keep open*. Accept approves the recommendation and starts no code change, command or external action. An accepted recommendation that needs work reads *Action agreed* and stays among what remains; only an explicit "no change needed" recommendation may close the thread on accept, its reason kept. Also mark resolved, dismiss with a required comment, and reopen. "Things not established" become *Verification gap* threads, tied to the related finding thread where there is one, never duplicated. Older saved findings stay readable and manageable without loss; without a recommendation they say so honestly and offer to request one; nothing is invented on migration and no provider call starts by itself. No Gerrit posting: threads are kept in DSpatch's own review data.
3. **Presentation and review status.** A muted amber palette for open threads (dark warm background, matte yellow-orange accent), the chosen thread clearly visible, its code marked by outline or margin mark that leaves the diff's red and green readable; status also in text or icon; closed threads dimmed but showable and reopenable. Separate review coverage from remaining questions ("30/30 changed lines reviewed by agent", "1 open question · 3 verification gaps"); no dominant "Whole part approved" while findings remain. Coverage, approval and the agent's verdict keep their meaning; a thread's status change approves no lines and rewrites no earlier result.
4. **Ask from a selection** in the walk's code panels, in the agent's explanation text in Discussion and Step (code in the explanation included) and in the threads, reusing the diff view's Explain this/Ask. Ask opens the question field with the selection quoted; the person writes and sends. Code carries file, revision, side and line range; explanation text carries the answer, walk step or thread it came from; a selection from a thread keeps the discussion in that thread. Unsent text is kept; no double menus, no accidental sending, the selection not lost when the field takes focus.
5. **Show new questions and answers.** Sending a question or choosing Explain this opens the right discussion and scrolls to the new entry and its answer area; while an answer streams, follow it when at the bottom, stop when the person scrolls up and offer a quiet "New response ↓". No forced scroll on ordinary re-renders; scroll the right panel; docked and on its own; plain questions, Explain this and threads.
6. **The code walk's first and last panels** are never activated by scrolling, so their highlight and explanation are missing until Next/Previous. Fix the activation so both ends can be reached, the active panel, highlight and explanation stay in step, the choice is stable with several panels visible, in both directions, at any zoom and window size, with one panel, content that needs no scroll and panels taller than the view; Next/Previous and direct choice keep working without the scroll handling choosing back.
7. **The workspace's other repositories as read context** for delegated review, walks, Explain this and Ask: the review's repository and revisions stay its scope; other repositories explicitly added to the workspace are readable context the agent can discover and read when needed, not sent whole. Read-only policy and content exclusion for every root; no access outside the workspace. Citations name repository, file and the version or snapshot actually read, and open from the review; context reading gives no coverage or approval. Establish the library version the reviewed code uses from its build and dependency information where possible, otherwise distinguish what the workspace shows from what is verified for the reviewed build; keep the review reproducible.
8. **One diff panel for one change in a walk step.** References to the old and new side of the same change share one diff presentation; each stays a narrative step whose choice moves the highlight and explanation to its side and lines. Separate regions or files stay separate. Before shows only the old version, After only the new, Before → After the comparison, and the headings say what is shown. Sources, coverage and navigation kept; saved walks get the better presentation without regeneration.

Verification: an isolated profile and a neutral test repository; targeted tests for the items above; the whole flow in real windows on Linux, docked and on its own, at several zoom levels, with desktop screenshots of docked views; Windows where available, otherwise stated; deterministic provider data for UI trials, and a bounded real-provider trial if available and allowed. Document the behaviour and commit. No release and no Gerrit publishing. User acceptance stays in Review.

## Implementation Notes

<!-- SECTION:NOTES:BEGIN -->
Started 5 November 2026 on Linux. Governing decisions read: VISION.md section 3.4 (the decided review flow; agent-generated walks; reading the code from review questions; short answers first and the panel; reading around a change and asking from any code; one rule for what is reviewed and the review's own panel), REVIEW_COVERAGE_AND_CODE_WALKS.md, INTERACTION_MODEL.md, AGENT_BEHAVIOUR_AND_ORCHESTRATION.md section 5, and DSPATCH_CONTENT_EXCLUSION_CONTRACT.md sections 5–6. Niclas's assignment is recorded as his decision in VISION.md section 3.4 ("Review threads, recommendations, Ask from every text, and the workspace as read context"); it changes where a thread question's answer is kept and what review agents may read (the other repositories of the workspace as read context).

What was built:
- Threads (`src/shared/review-threads.ts`): a thread is read from a done delegated review's result (findings gathered as before by title; gaps tied to a finding by its title, otherwise threads of their own; older `uncertainties` become gaps), with a stable id `<job>:f|g:<hash>`. The person's events (comment, accept, keep-open, resolve, dismiss with a required comment, reopen) are kept in `PairRecord.threads` under that id and written by the host (`reviewPair.thread`), so every window of the review gets them through the existing pair-changed event. Accept on a work-needing recommendation is *Action agreed* (still remaining); on `keep` it resolves. A thread status never touches marks, agent reviews, jobs or totals (tested).
- The agent's answer format (`reviewInstruction`, `readReviewAnswer`): `evidence`, `recommendation {kind, action, rationale, uncertainty, nextStep}` and `gaps` (with `finding`, optional place). `withCheckedFindings` keeps a place that is not at its commit as `missing` with its reason; it marks nothing.
- Diff window: thread cards inline under the last line of their first place in the file, the places marked with an inset margin mark (outline when chosen) that leaves the red and green readable; threads in folded code and file-level threads one line each above the diff; review-level threads in Files changed; "N open findings" on a file row opens the file at its first open thread; "1 open question · 3 verification gaps" beside the coverage line opens an overview. The delegated review result reads "N/M changed lines reviewed and approved by the agent" and what remains apart; its findings list and "Not established" list are replaced by one line per thread (each presented once).
- Walk window and the review's panel: the threads at the shown place in Step, the walk's delegated result with its threads opening in place, and choosing one shows it in the diff (`DiffLocation.threadId`).
- A question in a thread is sent through the review discussion with subject `{kind:'thread'}`; its envelope carries the thread as it stands and its places; the answer shows in the thread and the discussion shows one line pointing to it. A walk window's thread question gets its context from the pair.
- Ask: *Ask…* beside *Explain this* in the walk's offer; text selected in a thread is asked about in that thread (its own offer; the walk's offer ignores it, so there is no second menu); quotes of thread text are `source: 'thread'`. Unsent thread text is kept per thread while the window lives.
- The discussion scrolls its own scroller with the chat's DSP-58 rule (`discussionScrollReason` + `decideScroll`): a new question goes to the latest, an answer arriving is followed only at the bottom, scrolling up shows *New response ↓* above the question field.
- The walk map's end panes: `activationLine` slides the activation point to the edge at either end of the travel (`src/renderer/walk-activation.ts`), and at either end no extra travel is needed after a switch.
- Shared panes (`src/renderer/walk-panes.ts`, implementer report): neighbouring places of a walk in the same change area (Git's hunks on each side, widened by 3 lines) share one pane; each stays a station; the pane heading says Before / Before → After / After, or From / To for an unchanged file. Choice (for Niclas to confirm): only places next to each other in the walk join, so the map keeps the walk's order.
- Workspace context (`src/host/context-repositories.ts`, implementer report): the other Git repositories under the open workspace's folders are recorded per review (`userData/reviews/context/<key>.json`), written from Git objects into `<pair>/context/<name>/` at a resolved commit (submodule pin; declared version with a matching tag; path dependency → HEAD; otherwise HEAD, "not established"), with working-copy differences as counts only. Prompts describe them; citations are `{repository, path, start, end}`, checked with Git at the recorded commit, kept as `contextReferences`/`contextLocation`, never reviewed or approved, and open in place under an answer (`readContextFile`). Policies unchanged (context is inside the session folder); deny reasons mention `context/`.
- The SIMULATED presentation adapter: recommendations and evidence on its findings, and with `DSPATCH_REVIEW_SIM=threads` a removed-line question, a place that is not there, a keep recommendation and gaps; a discussion question answered from a context repository.
